# Recipe sources and attribution

This companion collection contains the 809 built-in Pantry Plate recipes: 14 starter recipes, 25 sourced Asian adaptations, and 770 additional TheMealDB imports.

The `CC-BY-4.0` license in this repository covers the Soft Studio illustrations. Recipe text and referenced photography retain their original source rights; they are not relicensed by the illustration release.

Each sourced recipe in `metadata/recipes.json` retains its `sourceUrl`, original ingredient names/measures and cooking steps. Expanded TheMealDB records also retain publisher/API links and per-row conversion assumptions under `normalization`. Sources without a separate publisher URL link to the corresponding TheMealDB recipe.

Recipe discovery used [TheMealDB public API](https://www.themealdb.com/api.php) on October 2, 2026. Source IDs are preserved in recipe IDs, and the app's previously reviewed source adaptations remain in the collection.

Planning quantities can include approximate conversions or suggested amounts where sources are vague. Reference servings and times may be estimated. Original source measures are provided separately for cooking. Dietary tags were not independently verified.

The recipe archive includes JSON records, ingredient mappings and the CC BY 4.0 illustration files. Recipe photographs are referenced through their existing image URLs; photograph files are not bundled. Source credits are visible in the website's recipe detail view.

Suggested illustration credit: **Soft Studio Ingredients by Alex Wang**, linking [the project](https://github.com/alextyhwang/soft-studio-ingredients) and [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Attribute recipe content to the linked original recipe/publisher separately.
