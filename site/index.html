From our pantry

This collection started as artwork for Pantry Plate. We’re sharing it as a standalone resource for designers and developers building with food.

The illustrations were AI-generated, then checked for transparent backgrounds, consistent styling, and recognizable subjects. They’re illustration assets, not photographs or scientific food-identification labels.
