What’s in the film

Individual ingredients cut rapidly in the center of the frame. A continuous pullback reveals all 520 images while several fruits keep swapping. The cuts gradually slow until the grid settles, followed by “520 ingredients. Free.” The film has synchronized taps, a short sweep, and a closing impact.
